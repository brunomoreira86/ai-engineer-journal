# Chapter 1 Exercise: Your "Why" + Job Spec Analysis

- **Objective:** Write your own honest "why AI engineering, why now" and ground it against real, current job market evidence instead of assumption.
- **Prerequisites:** None, this is the entry point.
- **Steps:**
  1. Write your personal "why" in raw form first, before looking at any job data. Don't edit for polish, capture it close to how it actually comes out. Keep every version; nothing here gets discarded, even the parts that don't end up in the final chapter.
  2. Define your actual target: location/remote policy, seniority band, and target comp. Be specific, "AI Engineer" alone is too broad to research against.
  3. Collect 10 real, current job postings matching that target. Pull full content, not just the title, you need the actual requirements list.
  4. For each posting, log: company, role, comp (if listed), location/remote policy, and every specific skill/tool/requirement named.
  5. Aggregate into a ranked skill-frequency list across all postings.
  6. Compare that list against whatever roadmap or assumptions you started with. Note what's overweighted, what's underweighted, and what's missing entirely.
  7. Fill in your own skill gap matrix: required-in-market vs. your current self-rated level (0-5) per skill.
- **Success criteria:** A completed skill gap matrix backed by real postings (not guesses), and at least one assumption from your original plan that the data actually changed.
- **Time estimate:** 2-3 hours.

**Reference output:** see `07_career_prep/SKILL_GAP_MATRIX.md` and `planning_journal/2026-08-13-job-market-skill-research.md` for how this played out. 9 of 10 target postings were reachable; the 10th was left out rather than guessed at.
