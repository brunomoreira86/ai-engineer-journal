# 2026-08-13 — Start a planning journal

**Context:** We're treating the AI Engineer Journey as a real software project (roadmap, sprint backlog in PROJECT_BOARD.csv, chapter-as-sprint structure). Bruno pointed out that the planning/PM process itself is worth learning and documenting deliberately, not just absorbed as a side effect of building.

**Decision:** Keep a lightweight, dated log of planning/PM decisions in `planning_journal/` inside this same repo, rather than starting a separate repo or book right away.

**Why:** It costs nothing to start now, and it captures decisions as they happen instead of reconstructing them from memory later. If this does become its own book, the raw material is already there in chronological order.

**Reconsider if:** the log grows large enough, or clearly diverges enough in voice/audience from the AI engineering manuscript, that it deserves to be spun out into its own repo/book. Also noted: the roadmap and project plan themselves are expected to shift over time (see feedback memory on collaboration style) — this journal is partly where we'll capture those pivots and the reasoning behind them.
