# Book Bible

- **Working title:** The AI Engineer's Journal
- **Narrator voice:** first-person, plain English, written as the learning happens (no hindsight-only polish)
- **Reader:** someone with dev/business background wanting a production AI Engineer job, not a research scientist role
- **Tone rule:** every chapter must contain one real failure or surprise — no chapter should read like a clean tutorial
- **Recurring thread:** using AskDila as the live testbed for every project where possible

## Chapter template: The Story / The Concept / The Build / The Debrief

- **The Story** — first-person narrative: what I was trying to do and why, written as it happened.
- **The Concept** — the underlying idea explained plainly, researched and verified against real sources.
- **The Build** — narrative of building the thing, but must include an explicit numbered step-by-step sub-section (not just prose) so a reader can actually follow along and reproduce what was built, not just read about it. This is the part that makes the chapter usable, not only readable.
- **The Debrief** — what broke, what surprised me, what I'd do differently, tied back to the chapter's status checklist.
